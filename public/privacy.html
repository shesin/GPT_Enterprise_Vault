Privacy

Draft. The owner must review this text and add a support e-mail address before the game is published in a store or with ads.

What the game stores

This version has no accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail or location. Your choices (board look, sound on/off, volume, move-hint setting, player names you type for a same-device game) are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.

Third parties

Background music is streamed from soundimage.org when you press Play; that site can see your IP address and the track requested. Fonts are served from this site. When advertising is added, the ad provider will be named here and a consent choice will be shown first where the law requires it.

Cookies

The game itself sets no cookies.
